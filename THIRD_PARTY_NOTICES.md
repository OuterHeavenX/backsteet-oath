| Room backgrounds, parallax layers, props, title backdrop, district map | `tools/art/gen_environments.py` | Project-owned |
| Weapons, icons, effects, pickups, UI, logo, app icon | `tools/art/gen_items_ui.py` (+ helper modules) | Project-owned |
