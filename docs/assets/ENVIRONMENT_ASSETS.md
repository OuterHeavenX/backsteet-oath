# Environment assets

All files below are procedurally generated by `tools/art/gen_environments.py`; original; project-owned.
No third-party, traced or ROM-derived imagery is used. The generator is pure Python 3 + Pillow + numpy,
deterministic (every RNG is seeded from room id / x position), and reads `game/data/rooms.json` as its
only input (read-only).

```
python3 tools/art/gen_environments.py                 # rebuild everything, then verify
python3 tools/art/gen_environments.py --rooms oq_kettle int_diner
python3 tools/art/gen_environments.py --only props    # rooms | parallax | props | title | map
python3 tools/art/gen_environments.py --verify        # check outputs against rooms.json
```

`--verify` checks: every room PNG exists at exactly `width × 360`; interiors are fully opaque; exteriors
are opaque in the ground band (y ≥ 214) and have no semi-transparent pixels; every door in rooms.json is
drawn at its x (opaque, high contrast against the facade beside it); water blockers read as water;
props.json covers every prop type used in rooms.json with a valid file + anchor; parallax layers are
960×220 with no tiling seam; title/map are opaque; map.json has the six district keys. The room build
also asserts that the doors it drew match rooms.json exactly.

Each facade `kind` is a parametric routine that fills its x-range (variant + seeded RNG for variation);
each ground `style` likewise. District lighting is applied as a grade over non-emissive pixels (warm
sunset for Old Quarter, neutral day for Canal Market, orange dusk for Ironworks, night for Neon Mile,
storm night for Floodgate, dim for the warehouse), then light pools, wet-ground reflections (neon
asphalt, wet road, rooftop) and a quantised bloom from emissive pixels (neon, lit windows, lamps).
Signs are rendered in an original chunky 5×7 pixel font; neon signs use 1px "tube" letters that glow.

Doors (rooms.json `doors`) are drawn centred on their x with the bottom at y=214: ~28×48 lit opening in
a frame, transom, step on the sidewalk and a small awning or lamp. Styles follow the facade (diner chrome,
garage steel door + lamp, civic double doors for the rec center, neon-trimmed glass for pharmacy/arcade,
industrial door for the canteen/switch house, shack door for the bait shop, an alley opening with a lit
far end for Big Mo's Alley). Rail tunnel doors are dark stone arches with rails running in.

Water blockers (`cm_canalwalk`) are painted as water at their exact rects with a back railing along the
far edge of the deck (y 252), a front edge beam + pilings (y 316) and quay ends at both sides, so the
walkable plank deck between them reads clearly.

## Parallax far layers (6)

`game/assets/bg/parallax_<district>.png`, 960×220, horizontally tileable (every element is drawn
wrapped), opaque. old_quarter (sunset sky + sun, rooftop skyline with water tanks/steeples),
canal_market (day sky, pixel clouds, hills, gabled town), ironworks (orange dusk, smokestacks with
plumes, gantry cranes, pylons), neon_mile (night, stars, moon, lit towers, faint neon haze), floodgate

rain-streaked windows, roof trusses, distant lamps — shows through the open truss band at the top of the
warehouse rooms).

## Props (57) + props.json

`game/assets/props/<type>.png` — 3/4 belt-scroller view, 1px `#1a1220` outline, light from the
upper-left, scaled for 46px characters. `game/assets/props/props.json` maps each type to
`{"file", "anchor", "footprint"}`. **Anchor** = bottom-centre of the base, using the same convention as
the character sheets (anchor (32, 60), feet on row 59): the anchor y equals the image height, so the
bottom outline row of the base sits just above the prop's ground position. **Footprint** = ground-plane
box spanning x ± w/2 and y − h … y; h = 0 means walk-through (traffic cone); poles get a tiny 6×4 box;

## Notes / interpretation of rooms.json

- `rw_*` rooms are `kind: exterior` but themed as the warehouse: `rw_dock` (loading bay under a canopy)
  and `rw_office` (ceiling with fluorescent panels) are fully opaque; `rw_storage` and `rw_floor` leave the
  top roof-truss band (y 0–40) transparent so the warehouse parallax (high windows) shows through.
- Rail tunnel doors are dark stone arches ~38×56 (slightly larger than the ~30×50 shop doors).
- Decorative doors (rowhouse stoops, theater, back doors) are drawn darker and closed, without the lit
  opening/awning/lamp, so the interactive doors from rooms.json stand out.
- `nm_backalley` is an exterior with a `south` exit: drawn as a lit alley mouth across x 280–360 in the
  foreground strip rather than an interior doormat.
- `cm_pier` `warehouse_small` variant 1 with sign DRAWBRIDGE is drawn as the bridge-tender's house plus
  the raised bascule leaf; it is a static background, so it still shows the leaf raised after the
  drawbridge exit unlocks.
- Sky gradients follow the art-bible stop order top→bottom.
